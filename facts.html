Knowledge saves lives

What epilepsy is — and what it is not.

50M people worldwide. 80% in LMICs. Up to 70% seizure-free with low-cost meds. Not contagious, not witchcraft.

Fast facts

What is epilepsy?

Chronic disorder of brain — recurrent unprovoked seizures due to abnormal electrical activity.

Causes

Prenatal/perinatal brain damage, congenital abnormalities, infections, stroke, tumors, febrile seizures, head injury. In Uganda, onchocerciasis link under research.

Treatment

70% seizure-free with anti-seizure meds. Surgery for drug-resistant. Discontinuation may be considered after 2 years free.

First aid during a seizure

  1. Stay calm, keep person safe, time seizure
  2. Do NOT put anything in mouth, do NOT restrain
  3. Turn on side to keep airway clear
  4. Stay until awake — if >5 min or repeats, seek help

Myths vs Facts

Myth: Contagious / cannot work-marry / witchcraft
Fact: Medical brain condition — people can live normal productive lives with treatment & support.

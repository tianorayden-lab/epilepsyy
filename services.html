Programs

Mobilize. Build. Advocate. Refer.

402 volunteers on bicycles, 7-member district assemblies, CBO registration, forum theatre in Acholi.

Mobilization & Sensitization

Persons with epilepsy, parents, guardians form support groups — sharing experience, solving problems together, sense of togetherness, income-generating skills.

Capacity Building

Elect leaders at sub-county & district, register as CBOs, training in advocacy, leadership, finance, reporting, group dynamics, records, mobilization, counseling. Transform lifestyles from poverty & stigma.

Lobbying & Advocacy

Right to regular medication, education, property. Engage districts & central government — epilepsy as priority, PWE as important citizens. Enhance branches to hold duty bearers accountable.

Counselling & Referral

Secretariat offers counselling on seizure management & positive living, info on education/employment, referral to public hospitals, documentation centre, magazine on topical issues.

Networking

Voices of Health Rights Coalition, NUDIPU umbrella, MoH, HURINET, NGO Forum, UNHCO, IAPO, International Bureau for Epilepsy — ESAU holds Vice Chair African Regional Commission IBE.

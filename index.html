What we do

A whole-person approach to epilepsy.

We mobilize, build capacity, advocate, and link people to care — so stigma drops and drug access rises.

Mobilization & Sensitization

Forming support groups where members share experience, reduce isolation, and learn income-generating activities.

Explore
Capacity Building

Training in leadership, advocacy, finance, records, counseling and group dynamics; CBO registration at sub-county.

Explore
Lobbying & Advocacy

Right to medication, education, property — holding duty bearers accountable at district and national level.

Explore
Counselling & Referral

Seizure management counselling, documentation centre, magazine publishing, hospital referrals via secretariat.

Explore
Research • Makerere University

Study reveals epilepsy prevalence and treatment barriers in Uganda — urgent need for awareness and resources

“We should increase drug availability in health facilities and make sure that a person with epilepsy is able to get medication whenever they need it. If everybody understands epilepsy it would be easier for treatment and stigma to go down.” — Dr. Nekesa. ESAU’s grassroots peer talks are building confidence and demanding inclusion in every community service.

HomeWho we are
Owned by persons with epilepsy

A national voice that began in the shadows — now shaping policy.

Members are owners. Sub-county → District → National. 7 members to District Assembly, 7 + 2 substitutes to National. Open to PWE, relatives, professionals.

Vision

A society where epilepsy is understood and persons with epilepsy live with dignity, participating fully.

Mission

Improve quality of life through awareness, advocacy, self-help groups and income-generating activities.

How membership works

Join with 5,000 UGX

Receives membership card from secretariat. Meet at least once a month at your health unit.

Why groups?

United voice for rights, pooled drug costs, government respect, visibility in planning — out of the shadows.

Forum Theatre for awareness

Interactive theatre for socially excluded groups. Staging shows in Acholi sub-region: Amuru, Gulu, Pader, Kitgum.

Focus for the future

  • Support all sub-county branches to register as Epilepsy Self Help Associations.
  • Support district & sub-county to develop fundable proposals.
